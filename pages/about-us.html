Our Story

Sine's Root & Care began in 2025, born from a simple belief: every person deserves hair care that is pure, affordable, and made right here at home.

Why I started

Sfter searching for natural hair care products that were both effective and reasonably priced, I decided to create my own. No harsh chemicals, no imported ingredients, just what our hair actually needs.

Our Mission

To provide quality, locally made hair care that nourishes from root to tip - making natural beauty accessible to everyone.

Our Vision

To grow, create local jobs, and empower more peeople to embrace their natural hair with confidence.

What Makes Us Different

  • Natural ingredients - no harmful addictives
  • Proudly made in South Africa
  • Fair prices - quality shouldn't cost a fortune
  • Made with love, by hand
