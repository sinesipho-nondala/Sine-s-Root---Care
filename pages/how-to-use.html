How to Use Our Products

Simple steps to get the very best from your natural hair care routine 🌿

Growth Oil

  1. Apply a few drops directly to your scalp
  2. Massage gently in circular motions for 2–3 minutes to boost circulation
  3. Leave on for at least 30 minutes — or overnight with a silk cap
  4. Rinse thoroughly with a gentle shampoo

Use 2–3 times per week for best results

Moisture Butter

  1. Take a small amount and rub between your palms until soft
  2. Apply evenly to damp hair, focusing on the ends
  3. Leave in for 15–20 minutes
  4. Rinse out — or leave in for lighter styles

Perfect for daily moisture and soft coils

Scalp Treatment

  1. Apply directly to clean scalp
  2. Massage gently for 1–2 minutes to spread evenly
  3. Leave on for 10–15 minutes
  4. Rinse with lukewarm water

Great before your wash day routine

General Hair Care Tips

  • ✅ Use products consistently — natural care takes time to show results
  • ✅ Eat well & stay hydrated — healthy hair starts from within
  • ✅ Limit heat styling to protect your natural texture
  • ✅ Avoid harsh chemical treatments that strip moisture
  • ✅ Protect hair from sun exposure with a hat or scarf
  • ✅ Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase to reduce breakage
