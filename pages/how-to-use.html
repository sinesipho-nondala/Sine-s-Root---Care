How to Use Our Products

Learn how to get the most out of our natural beauty products.

Growth Oil

  1. Apply a few drops of Growth Oil to your scalp.
  2. Massage gently for 2-3 minutes to stimulate blood flow.
  3. Leave it on for at least 30 minutes or overnight for best results.
  4. Rinse thoroughly with a mild shampoo.

Moisture Butter

  1. Take a small amount of Moisture Butter and rub it between your palms.
  2. Apply evenly to damp hair, focusing on the ends.
  3. Leave it in for 15-20 minutes, then rinse or style as desired.

Scalp Treatment

  1. Apply the Scalp Treatment directly to your scalp.
  2. Massage gently for 1-2 minutes to ensure even distribution.
  3. Leave it on for 10-15 minutes before rinsing with lukewarm water.

General Hair Care Tips

  • Use our products consistently for the best results.
  • Maintain a healthy diet and stay hydrated to support hair health.
  • Avoid excessive heat styling and harsh chemical treatments.
  • Protect your hair from sun exposure and environmental damage.
