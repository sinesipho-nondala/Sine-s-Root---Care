Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products 100% natural?

Yes! Every product is handcrafted with pure, organic, African-sourced ingredients. We never use sulfates, parabens, mineral oil, or synthetic fragrances.

Can children use these products?

Absolutely — our gentle formulas are suitable for the whole family. For very young children, we recommend a patch test on a small area first.

How long until I see results?

Healthy hair takes time! Most people notice softer, stronger, shinier hair within 2–4 weeks. Consistent use shows best results — give it 6–8 weeks for visible growth.

Do you deliver across South Africa?

Yes! We deliver nationwide. Orders take 3–5 working days to arrive, and you’ll get a tracking number to follow your package.
